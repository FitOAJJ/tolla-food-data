Tolla food data

Packaged-food nutrition for on-phone search in the Tolla app, built once a month from Open Food Facts. The app downloads these files by itself; there is nothing to do here.

  • manifest.json: which packs exist, their month, size and checksum.
  • Build code and monthly releases: github.com/FitOAJJ/tolla-food-data.
